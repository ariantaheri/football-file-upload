# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour
- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.
- Give clear context when reporting problems or proposing changes.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility
Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may block participation when necessary to protect the community. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through the contact method listed on the repository profile.
